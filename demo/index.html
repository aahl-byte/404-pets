404 Pets — demo page

This page previews the pet without installing the extension. A little cat should appear at the bottom of the window. Things to try:

  • Wait — it walks, sits, naps, and explores on its own.
  • Wave the cursor quickly near it — it may give chase and pounce.
  • Click it — hearts!
  • Drag and toss it — it goes flying and lands on its feet.
  • Watch it climb — it jumps onto headings, images, and buttons on the page, and paws at them.

A heading it can sit on

Cats sit on anything. Keyboards, laundry, this paragraph's heading.

Another perch over here

Scroll around — if the cat is standing on something when it scrolls away, it rides along and hops off at the edge of the window.

In the real extension, the toolbar popup lets you toggle the pet, change its coat color, and toss it a ball of yarn.
